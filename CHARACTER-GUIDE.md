# REDPOLY Character Bible — Monsoon Mischief

## Creative direction
Redesign all four dolls as original, collectible creature mascots with the fun, readable appeal of a creature-collecting adventure game. **Do not copy Pokémon or any other existing franchise**—no recognizable species, silhouettes, markings, poses, logos, Poké Balls, or signature motifs. Each REDPOLY creature must have its own unmistakable silhouette and exact repeatable model details.

The Indian connection is through the names, monsoon/night/nature palette, and contemporary warmth—not costume shorthand. Avoid sacred/deity/temple imagery, religious symbols, flags, and stereotyped human clothing.

## Shared rendering rules
- Soft 3D collectible vinyl figure, highly polished but still graphic and toy-like; smooth molded surface, gentle studio lighting, crisp dark contour, bright poppy colors.
- Single character, full body, centered, straight-on or gentle 3/4 view; clean silhouette with margin.
- **Deliver as a real transparent-alpha PNG.** No checkerboard baked into pixels, white/colored backdrop, floor, scenery, cast shadow, label, text, logo, or watermark.
- Canonical identity rule: create one master PNG for each character. Reuse that exact file for hero, product card, collections, and packaging mockups; never independently regenerate alternate versions for different page placements. Keep every named feature and color blocking fixed.

## 01 — BARSAAT | The Curious One
**Name cue:** Barsaat means rain.
**Silhouette:** compact, pear-shaped amphibious creature; wide rounded head flowing into a small soft body; two short arms, two squat feet, one long curled tail ending in a single three-lobed droplet fin. No dinosaur plates, horns, or wings.
**Face:** exactly two large dark-teal oval eyes, small curious smile, three tiny aqua freckles under the left eye.
**Crest:** exactly three rounded raindrop-shaped crest lobes, centered on top of the head.
**Palette:** vivid aqua body `#19C9D2`; deep teal back/fin `#087F91`; bright lime belly patch `#C8FF00`; small mango-orange cheek dot `#FF6B1A`.
**Personality:** curious, buoyant, welcoming. No umbrella, clothes, star patch, or extra props.

## 02 — AGNI | The Chaotic One
**Name cue:** Agni means fire; this is an original fire creature, not a religious depiction.
**Silhouette:** energetic upright ember imp with a round body, two short arms, two sturdy feet, two asymmetrical flame-shaped ears, and one large curling flame tail. No goggles, wings, horns, or spiky hair.
**Face:** exactly two bright golden eyes, one mischievous open grin, three tiny gold freckles on each cheek.
**Flame details:** one tall flame crest between the ears; smooth flame forms, not sharp spikes.
**Palette:** poppy orange `#FF5B19`; marigold flame tips `#FFC928`; hot-pink belly mark `#FF4E92`; small electric-violet accents `#7855E8`.
**Personality:** exuberant and playfully chaotic. No weapons, religious marks, clothes, or extra props.

## 03 — NOOR | The Mysterious One
**Name cue:** Noor means light.
**Silhouette:** small floating moth-like night creature; rounded indigo body, two short feet, exactly two broad crescent-shaped wings held open, and two short curved antennae each ending in one round glow bulb. No hood, crown, cloak, or extra limbs.
**Face:** exactly two warm golden glowing eyes; no visible mouth.
**Chest mark:** one small pale-gold crescent, centered.
**Palette:** electric indigo `#3C38B8`; midnight violet `#1B164D`; vivid fuchsia wing edges `#E84AB7`; glowing mango gold `#FFD23F`.
**Personality:** gentle, magical, mysterious rather than scary. No stars, constellations, religious symbols, or props.

## 04 — AARAV | The Gentle Giant
**Name cue:** Aarav is a contemporary Indian name associated with calm.
**Silhouette:** broad, low, friendly stone-and-moss creature; large rounded pebble-shaped body, four short legs, one small blunt cream nose horn, and one oversized rounded shell/back plate divided into exactly five smooth overlapping teal plates. No rhino frill, dinosaur spikes, or tail.
**Face:** exactly two small kind eyes and a calm closed smile; no sharp teeth or aggressive brows.
**Nature marks:** exactly three bright leaf-shaped lime patches on the left side of the back plate; one warm-cream belly.
**Palette:** vivid peacock teal `#12B8B2`; deeper teal plates `#087F80`; bright leaf lime `#9DEB32`; warm cream `#F4F0E6`; tiny mango accent `#FFB22E`.
**Personality:** patient, safe, dependable. No armor, clothes, or props.

## Production checklist
1. Generate one canonical full-body transparent PNG per character, using only its exact definition above.
2. Review silhouette, feature counts, palette and alpha channel against this guide before use.
3. Reuse those four master PNGs everywhere in the site; packaging and collection designs may add graphic framing around the unchanged master art, never redraw the figure.
