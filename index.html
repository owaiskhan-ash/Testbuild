Every piece begins in the hands of a maker we know by name. We travel from workshop to workshop, gathering small batches of honest goods, so that each one arrives at your door with its story intact.

Two generations share one bench in our studio. Old techniques are passed along by hand, new ideas are welcomed with a grin, and nothing leaves the table until both agree it is ready. That patience is what you feel when you hold the finished piece — made to be used, loved and kept for years.
